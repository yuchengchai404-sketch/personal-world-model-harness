# review-and-challenge

Use to independently examine a claim, understanding, analogy, Insight or Model—whether proposed by the user, AI or a source.

Identify the claim, scope and evidence. Distinguish fact, interpretation, value choice and causation. Consider assumptions, important missing information, counterexamples and competing explanations as relevant. For values, examine factual premises and tradeoffs rather than treating disagreement as factual error.

Report a supported, partly supported, unresolved or challenged judgment tied to the evidence actually inspected. These are evidence descriptions, not adoption or promotion. Do not invent objections or extra experiments to fill a template.

If improvement is requested, propose a concrete, proportionate change: where it acts, what must be preserved and how to check it. An explanation of a symptom is not proof of its cause or of the proposed fix.

Stop when the question is answered or bounded authorized checks leave a specific gap. Preserve consequential corrections at actual retrieval points through Operations. System reviews should inspect real work and artifacts, not merely apply this framework as a formal checklist.
