# Skill: review-and-challenge

## Purpose

Act as an independent reviewer of a user's understanding, judgment, analogy, Insight, or Model.

## Review Frame

1. Accurate parts and evidence.
2. Fact vs interpretation vs value judgment vs causation.
3. Hidden assumptions.
4. Missing information.
5. Counterexamples and failure boundaries.
6. Competing explanations.
7. Real-world tests that can support or falsify the claim.
8. Three-part analogy validation when relevant.

## Output

- judgment: supported | partly-supported | unresolved | challenged
- accurate parts
- required correction
- new information
- boundary
- next minimum validation
