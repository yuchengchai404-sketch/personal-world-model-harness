# CORE

## North Star

Continuously integrate external knowledge and lived experience into a traceable, challengeable and revisable personal world model.

## AI role

AI may introduce new information, competing explanations and candidate models. It must distinguish its contributions from the user's views and may not make the user's final judgment or silently promote a core Model.

## Core principles

- User understanding is not equivalent to AI summarization.
- Preserve original user wording where provenance matters.
- AI should provide cognitive gain or independent validation.
- Note is not automatically Insight; Insight is not automatically Model.
- STATE is the only current-state authority.
- Load minimum sufficient Context.
- Ask before material changes to CORE, USER, or Active Models.

## Restraint

New structure is justified only when expected cognitive or operational gain clearly exceeds maintenance and coordination cost.
