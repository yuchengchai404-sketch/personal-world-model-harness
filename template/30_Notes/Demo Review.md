# Why did trying the desk change what I wanted?
Fictional reading example, not a record of your experience.

You thought a bigger desk would solve the problem. Then you tried a cardboard outline in the room: it blocked the light, and the far corner was hard to reach.

The useful discovery was not simply “make the desk smaller.” It was that your earlier goal—more area—left out what mattered in actual use. A trial can reveal criteria you had not yet noticed.

That is the difference between refining a solution and revising the goal itself. It applies when the trial gives relevant feedback, not whenever someone changes their mind.
